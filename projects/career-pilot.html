Gnana's Career Pilot

An AI helper for job hunting — it finds roles that fit you, rewrites your résumé for each job, helps you apply, and keeps your applications organised.

About this project

Looking for a job usually means jumping between many websites: job boards, your résumé, spreadsheets, and LinkedIn messages. It’s messy, and “auto-apply” tools often spam companies or get accounts banned.

Gnana's Career Pilot is a personal app that does the heavy lifting for you — and still lets you check everything before you submit. It runs on your computer (or in the browser), so your data stays with you.

What it does

  • Pulls job listings from popular job sites in one place
  • Scores how well each job matches your résumé — and explains why
  • Writes résumé bullets, cover letters, and screening answers for that role
  • Helps fill application forms — you review and click submit
  • Tracks applications, follow-ups, learning goals, and tips from an AI coach
